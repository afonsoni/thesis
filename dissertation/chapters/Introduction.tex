\section{Context}

In recent years, Large Language Models (LLMs) have transformed natural language processing (NLP) \cite{openai2024gpt4technicalreport, yang2023harnessingpowerllmspractice, NEURIPS2022_b1efde53}, excelling in tasks like chatbots, summarization, and content analysis. However, they often struggle with domain-specific questions, producing inaccurate or irrelevant results, especially when data is outdated or sparse \cite{kandpal2023largelanguagemodelsstruggle, rag-survey}. This is particularly problematic in political discourse, where positions evolve over time.

Political party manifestos are crucial documents that outline a party's policy positions and ideological stances. These texts play a significant role in shaping public discourse and informing policy decisions \cite{manifestos-nz}. In the case of Portugal, like many other countries, understanding the nuances within these documents is crucial for informed civic participation \cite{marino-etal-2024-decoding}. However, the complexity and length of these documents can make them difficult for the average citizen and even researchers to navigate and fully understand.


\section{Motivation}

Citizens and researchers often struggle to navigate and compare political party programs, especially during elections. Existing tools either lack interpretability or require significant manual effort \cite{laver_benoit_garry, manifestos-nz}. This project aims to bridge this gap by leveraging RAG-based LLMs to retrieve and synthesize relevant information, particularly in Portuguese, where research on this issue remains limited.

Moreover, political divisions are deepening, often fueled by a lack of accessible information \cite{eleitorado}. A tool that helps citizens better understand party positions could contribute to a more informed electorate and mitigate misinformation.

The primary objective of this research is to investigate the effectiveness of RAG in retrieving and synthesizing political text content, focusing on Portuguese electoral programs. Specifically, the main objectives will cover:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{RAG Implementations:} Explore existing RAG implementations and their effectiveness for political manifesto analysis.
    \item \textbf{Challenges of RAG:} Identify challenges in applying RAG, particularly to Portuguese manifestos.
    \item \textbf{Solutions to Challenges:} Examine techniques to improve retrieval accuracy and relevance, such as optimized chunking strategies, hybrid search methodologies, and reranking retrieved content based on contextual importance and semantic coherence.

Expected results include a comprehensive comparison between different RAG implementations, insights into optimizing retrieval for complex political documents, and recommendations for integrating AI-driven techniques into political text analysis frameworks. Additionally, this research will also provide an experimental prototype demonstrating the retrieval and synthesis of political program content.

Given the time constraints, the development of a RAG-powered political quiz could serve as an additional feature to improve the quality of political quizzes that emerge during electoral periods, which often lack depth and accuracy. While this remains an optional objective rather than a core requirement, it could contribute to a better-informed political decision-making by offering a more reliable and nuanced analysis of party positions.

\section{Research Methods}

This research follows an experimental and analytical approach to assess the effectiveness of RAG-based retrieval for Portuguese political manifestos. The methodology consists of the following key steps:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Literature Review:} Conduct a systematic review of existing research on RAG pipelines, political text analysis, and related challenges in NLP.
    \item \textbf{Dataset Compilation:} Collect and preprocess the latest Portuguese political manifestos, ensuring consistency for retrieval.
    \item \textbf{Development:}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{RAG Experimentation:} Experiment with different RAG configurations, such as chunking strategies, hybrid search techniques, and reranking methods, to optimize retrieval accuracy.
        \item \textbf{Prototype Development:} Develop a functional prototype demonstrating the retrieval and synthesis of political content.
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Testing \& Evaluation:}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Quantitative methods:} Measure performance using evaluation metrics (e.g., hit rate, mean reciprocal rank or relevancy)
        \item \textbf{Qualitative methods:} Evaluate the interpretability and accuracy of generated summaries.
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Refinement:} Improve the prototype based on evaluation results, addressing identified issues and enhancing usability. This phase may also explore the integration of a political quiz feature.
\end{itemize}

By following this methodology, the study aims to validate the proposed hypothesis and provide actionable insights for improving AI-driven political text analysis.
